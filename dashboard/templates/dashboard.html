Takeaway

Both pages can look identical, but they are different origins. The real site stores a one-way hash and issues a session; the spoof validates nothing and keeps nothing. Every defense that actually stops phishing — password managers, passkeys, and reading the address bar — keys off the origin, not how the page looks.
