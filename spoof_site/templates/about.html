About this demonstration

This site is a look-alike of an online game store, built for a cybersecurity class. It is not the real store. The real store runs at a different web address:

Real store
{{ legit_origin }} This look-alike
{{ origin }}

The two pages can look identical, but they are different websites. The address in the browser's address bar — the origin — is what actually tells them apart. Spoofed sites copy how a page looks; they cannot copy the real site's address, and the defenses that matter are tied to that address:

  • A password manager only offers to fill a saved login on the exact site where you saved it, so it stays silent on a look-alike.
  • A passkey is bound to the real site's address and won't work on another.
  • HTTPS proves you are connected to whatever address is shown — not that the address belongs to the real company.

For the demonstration, this site stores nothing you type. The login and signup forms record only that a submission happened and then reject it; no email or password is saved.
